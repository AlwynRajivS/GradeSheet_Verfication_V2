Calculation Details: GPA = (Σ Credit × Grade Point for Earned Courses) ÷ Credit Earned (per semester, shown to 3 decimal places). CGPA = (Σ Credit × Grade Point for All Earned Courses up to selected session) ÷ Total Credit Earned (shown to 2 decimal places). Performance bands are a neutral CGPA grouping for internal review only — they do not represent any university's official classification.

Student verification sheets

GPA Calculation: GPA = (Σ Credit × Grade Point for Earned Courses) ÷ Credit Earned per semester (shown to 3 decimal places). CGPA Calculation: CGPA = (Σ Credit × Grade Point for All Earned Courses) ÷ Total Credit Earned up to and including the selected session (shown to 2 decimal places). Credit earned and grade point earned are calculated only for passed courses (excluding U, UA, RA grades). Cumulative figures use the most recent passing attempt per course up to the selected session (repeated arrears not double-counted).
